# Kids · Rewards

The Rewards tab is where a child spends the **reward points** they earn from
completing missions on real‑life rewards their parent created (like ice cream,
screen time or a movie night).

## What's on the Rewards tab
- **Available points** — the child's current spendable reward‑point balance.
- **Reward cards** — each shows the reward name, description and its XP/point
  cost.
- **Redeem** — if the child has enough points, they can claim a reward.

## How redeeming works
1. The child taps **Redeem** on a reward they can afford.
2. The claim goes to the parent for approval (points are held).
3. The parent **approves** and gives the real‑world reward.
4. The child confirms with **"Got it!"** to complete the exchange.

The Rewards tab respects the parent's **Rewards** feature toggle in
[Parent Controls](../parent/Settings.md).

## Availability by mode

| Capability | Free Trial | Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Browse rewards & see point balance | ✅ | ✅ |
| Redeem rewards (with parent approval) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Custom family rewards created by the parent | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Recurring & weekend‑challenge rewards** | — | ✅ (parent creates these on a paid plan) |

**Free Trial mode:** kids can browse and redeem any rewards the parent has
created during the trial.

**Subscription mode:** parents can additionally offer **recurring** and
**weekend‑challenge** rewards, giving kids more to earn toward.
