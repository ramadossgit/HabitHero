# Kids · Progress

The Progress tab shows a child how far their hero has come — a motivating view of
their XP, level and streaks.

## What's on the Progress tab
- **Level & XP** — current hero level and progress toward the next level.
- **Weekly progress** — how many missions were completed across the week.
- **Streaks** — consecutive days of completed habits.
- **Badges / achievements** — milestones the hero has unlocked.

This mirrors, in a kid‑friendly way, the data parents see in
[Parent → Progress](../parent/Progress.md).

## Availability by mode

| Capability | Free Trial | Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Level, XP & weekly progress | ✅ | ✅ |
| Streak tracking | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Streak bonuses & achievement badges** | Basic during trial | ✅ Full |

**Free Trial mode:** kids can see their level, XP and weekly progress during the
trial.

**Subscription mode:** unlocks the full **streak bonuses & achievements** system
(part of the premium feature set) for extra motivation.
